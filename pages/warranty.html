Warranty Policy

We stand behind every repair we do — 100%.

🛡️

30-Day Repair Warranty

On all parts replaced and all labour performed. No questions asked.

What's Covered

  • ✓ The specific component replaced during your repair
  • ✓ Labour costs for any re-repair of the same issue
  • ✓ Custom PC builds: 1–3 years depending on package selected
  • ✓ Software issues resulting directly from our OS or software services

What's Not Covered

  • ✗ Physical damage occurring after repair (drops, cracks, dents)
  • ✗ Liquid damage after the repair is completed
  • ✗ Tampered devices (opened by another party after repair)
  • ✗ Unrelated issues or new faults not present at time of repair
  • ✗ Consumables (thermal paste application, cleaning) beyond 30 days

How to Claim Warranty

Simply bring your device back with your original receipt or ticket number. We'll assess the issue and repair it at no charge if it falls within the warranty terms. Warranty claims must be made within 90 days of the original repair date.
