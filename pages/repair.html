FAQ

Common Questions

How does the ticket system work? ▼

When you submit the repair form, your details are instantly saved to our repair database and assigned a unique ticket number (e.g. CGZ-58291). You can use this ticket on the Track Order page to see real-time status updates, and it appears in Repair History for a full record of all your repairs.

How long does a repair take? ▼

Most common repairs are done within 24 hours. Complex jobs like motherboard-level work may take 2–3 business days. An estimated ready date is shown on your tracking page once diagnosis is complete.

Do I need an appointment? ▼

Walk-ins are always welcome during opening hours. Booking online (this form) helps us prepare parts in advance and reduces your wait time. The submitted ticket is logged immediately to our system.

Can I export my repair records? ▼

Yes! Visit the Repair History page and click "Export CSV" to download a spreadsheet of all your repairs. This matches the layout of our CasualGamerz_RepairDB.xlsx database file.

Is there a warranty on repairs? ▼

All repairs come with a 30-day warranty on parts replaced and labour performed. If the same issue returns within 30 days, we fix it at no charge. See our Warranty Policy for full details.
