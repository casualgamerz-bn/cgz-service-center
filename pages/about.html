About Casual Gamerz

Founded by gamers, for gamers. We know how much your setup means — that's why we take every repair personally.

Our Story

Started From a Garage

What started as two friends fixing consoles in a garage back in 2018 has grown into Brunei's most trusted gaming repair center. We opened our first physical store in Gadong in 2020, and haven't looked back since.

We understand the frustration of a broken PC mid-tournament or a PS5 that won't turn on the week of a game launch. That's why we prioritize speed without sacrificing quality — and why we offer a 90-day warranty on every single repair.

Today, our team of 8 certified technicians handles everything from controller drift to full custom build rigs, serving thousands of satisfied customers across Brunei.
